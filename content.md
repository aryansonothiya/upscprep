## 📝 The Hindu Editorial Analysis

*   **Context:** Analysis of the RBI's record ₹2.87 lakh crore surplus transfer to the Union government and its implications.
*   **Key Takeaways:**
    *   The significant surplus transfer highlights the RBI's growing role in supporting government finances, raising questions about central bank independence.
    *   Earnings from reserve management and foreign asset operations contribute substantially to this fiscal support.
    *   Concerns are raised about increasing fiscal centralisation, as these transfers are not part of the divisible pool for States, potentially impacting fiscal federalism.
    *   Stresses the need for transparency and accountability in such transfers to maintain the RBI's credibility and autonomy.

## 🏛️ Polity & Governance

*   **Current Issue:** ⚖️ Uniform Civil Code (UCC) Bill in Madhya Pradesh
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister Mohan Yadav announced that a UCC Bill will be tabled in the upcoming Monsoon Session of the State Legislative Assembly in July.
    *   A six-member high-level committee, headed by retired Supreme Court judge Justice Ranjana Prasad Desai, has drafted the Bill, after studying models from other BJP-ruled states like Uttarakhand and Gujarat.
    *   The move aligns with the BJP's long-standing agenda to implement a UCC across the country.

*   **Current Issue:** 🧒 Child Protection & School Safety Audit in Delhi
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Delhi Lieutenant-Governor (L-G) Taranjit Singh Sandhu ordered a comprehensive, citywide audit of all schools to ensure compliance with the provisions of the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act.
    *   The audit aims to assess security measures, staff vetting, and child-friendly reporting mechanisms to prevent and address sexual abuse.
    *   The initiative emphasizes the government's commitment to creating safer educational environments for children.

*   **Current Issue:** 🚨 SC Seeks Reply on Bail Pleas of 'Indian Mujahideen' Operatives
*   **Main Points:**
    *   The Supreme Court has sought a reply from Delhi Police regarding the bail pleas of two alleged Indian Mujahideen operatives detained under the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA).
    *   This case highlights judicial scrutiny on prolonged detentions under UAPA and concerns related to speedy trial and due process.
    *   It brings into focus the balance between national security imperatives and the rights of individuals accused of terror-related offenses.

*   **Current Issue:** 📚 Accountability for Errors in Odisha School Textbooks
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Odisha Chief Minister Mohan Charan Majhi ordered immediate corrective measures and action against officials responsible for numerous errors found in newly issued school textbooks (Classes 1-8).
    *   Reports indicated thousands of mistakes, including factual inaccuracies and incorrect imagery.
    *   A three-member panel will be constituted to probe the matter and recommend actions, underscoring the importance of quality education materials and accountability in governance.

## 🌍 Geography & Environment

*   **Current Issue:** 🌊 New Padma Barrage Project in Delhi
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Delhi is set to construct a new Padma barrage, located 180 km downstream of the Farakka Barrage, as a multipurpose project.
    *   The project aims to improve water supply to Delhi, generate hydropower, and control floods in the region.
    *   Concerns exist regarding its potential environmental impact on river ecosystems and groundwater levels, especially for downstream Bangladesh.
    *   Highlights the need for careful environmental impact assessments and regional cooperation for large-scale infrastructure projects on shared rivers.

*   **Current Issue:** 💧 Water Security for 'Viksit Bharat'
*   **Main Points:**
    *   An editorial emphasizes that robust water security is fundamental to achieving the vision of a 'Viksit Bharat' (Developed India).
    *   Calls for a comprehensive approach to water management, including addressing supply-side challenges, managing demand, ensuring water quality, and reforming governance.
    *   Stresses the importance of community participation, inter-state cooperation for shared water resources, and data-driven policies.
    *   Highlights the need for integrated watershed management, revival of traditional water bodies, and efficient irrigation practices to ensure sustainable water availability.

## 🌐 International Relations (IR)

*   **Current Issue:** 🤝 Iran-U.S. Framework Nuclear Deal & Financial Relief
*   **Main Points:**
    *   A 14-point framework agreement has been outlined, with Iran pledging not to produce nuclear weapons and to down-blend highly enriched uranium under IAEA supervision.
    *   The U.S. has agreed to facilitate the release of frozen Iranian assets, lift sanctions on Iranian products and services (including crude oil and banking), and withdraw naval blockade.
    *   The deal aims to create a plan for Iran's economic development, involving $300 billion, and contribute to regional peace.
    *   The agreement, expected to be signed in Geneva, has significant implications for global oil prices and maritime security, especially for Indian shipowners.

*   **Current Issue:** 🇮🇳🇺🇸 Modi-Trump Meeting at G7 Summit
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Prime Minister Narendra Modi met U.S. President Donald Trump on the sidelines of the G7 Summit in Evian, France.
    *   PM Modi raised the crucial issue of the safety of Indian seafarers working on global maritime trade routes, including the Strait of Hormuz, in light of recent incidents.
    *   Modi commended Trump's efforts to restore peace in West Asia, and both leaders affirmed the importance of keeping the Strait of Hormuz open for global economy.
    *   Trump praised Modi as a "very tough negotiator" and expressed that the US and India are "very close" to finalising a trade deal.

*   **Current Issue:** 🇮🇳🇷🇺 India-Russia Logistics Support Agreement (RELOS)
*   **Main Points:**
    *   The Reciprocal Exchange of Logistics Agreement (RELOS) between India and Russia, operationalised in January, defines procedures for reciprocal logistic support.
    *   It covers supplies, repair, fuel, port calls of warships, and use of airspace and airfield facilities by military aircraft for joint exercises, training, and Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief (HADR) situations.
    *   Crucially, the agreement *does not* provide for the permanent basing of troops but allows for temporary access during mutually agreed engagements.
    *   RELOS is similar to LSAs India has with nine other countries (e.g., US, UK, France) and notably grants India access to Russian military facilities in the Arctic, opening new avenues for cooperation.

*   **Current Issue:** 🇮🇳🇬🇧 India-U.K. Trade Deal Implementation
*   **Main Points:**
    *   The India-U.K. bilateral trade deal is set to take effect from July 15, aiming to significantly boost trade and investment between the two nations.
    *   The agreement seeks to cut tariffs on a wide range of goods and services, fostering deeper economic ties.
    *   It represents a culmination of years of negotiations and is expected to provide new opportunities for businesses and consumers in both countries.
