*   **Topic:** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 A.P.'s incentive for large families is misplaced (Opinion, Page 3)

*   **Key Takeaways:**

## 🌍 Geography & Environment
*   **Current Issue:** 🏞️ Dhaka's new Padma barrage will reshape water power in the region (Page 22)
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Bangladesh signed off on the new **Padma barrage** (Ganga in Bangladesh), located 180 km downstream of India's **Farakka barrage**.
    *   This project aims to control the Padma river and abate seasonal water scarcity, but experts warn of significant **environmental costs**, including altered river character, ecology, groundwater recharge, and increased salinity in the Sundarbans.
    *   The initiative highlights **transboundary water disputes**, particularly concerning the 1996 Ganga Water Treaty and the impact of large infrastructure projects on shared river basins and ecosystems, with potential **geopolitical implications** involving India and China.

*   **Current Issue:** 🚀 The universe's expansion still accelerating: researchers (Page 22)
*   **Main Points:**
    *   New research, analyzing data from Type Ia supernovae, has confirmed that the **universe is expanding at an accelerated rate**, reaffirming the existence of **dark energy**.
    *   This study rebuts previous research suggesting a weakening of dark energy and a deceleration of cosmic expansion.
    *   Type Ia supernovae serve as "cosmic mile markers," crucial for calculating vast cosmic distances and understanding the **structure and evolution of the universe**.

*   **Current Issue:** 🌿 India to meet 3% compressed biogas blending goal for FY27 (Page 13)
*   **Main Points:**
    *   India is on track to achieve its target of **3% Compressed Biogas (CBG) blending** with natural gas by FY27.
    *   This initiative promotes the use of **clean energy sources**, reduces reliance on fossil fuels, and supports the government's push for sustainable energy solutions.
    *   Meeting this target involves investments in CBG plants and infrastructure development, contributing to **energy security** and **environmental sustainability**.

## 🌐 International Relations (IR)
*   **Current Issue:** 🤝 Modi meets Trump, raises safety of Indian seafarers (Page 3)
*   **Main Points:**
    *   PM Modi met U.S. President Trump on the sidelines of the **G7 Summit in Evian, France**.
    *   Modi raised concerns about the **safety of Indian seafarers** in global maritime routes, including the Strait of Hormuz, following the killing of three Indian seafarers in a U.S. strike.
    *   Discussions also covered **restoring peace in West Asia**, keeping the Strait of Hormuz open for world economy, and the finalization of an **India-U.S. trade deal**.

*   **Current Issue:** 📜 Iran-U.S. framework deal outlines nuclear pledge, financial relief (Page 3, 14)
*   **Main Points:**
    *   A 14-point framework agreement between Tehran and Washington, expected to be signed in Geneva, outlines Iran's pledge not to produce nuclear weapons and maintaining a status quo on its nuclear program.
    *   The U.S. agreed to facilitate the release of **frozen Iranian assets**, create a plan for Iran's economic development ($300 billion), lift sanctions on products and services, and withdraw forces from "surrounding areas."
    *   Iran, in turn, pledged to restore pre-war traffic through the **Strait of Hormuz** and not produce nuclear weapons. This signifies a major step towards **de-escalation and potential economic integration** for Iran.

*   **Current Issue:** 🚢 What does the India-Russia logistics agreement allow? (Page 10)
*   **Main Points:**
    *   The **Reciprocal Exchange of Logistics Agreement (RELOS)** between India and Russia was operationalized in January.
    *   Similar to other **Logistics Support Agreements (LSAs)** India has with nine countries (U.S., U.K., France, etc.), RELOS defines procedures for reciprocal use of military facilities (bases, ports, airfields) for supplies, repair, and fuel during joint exercises, training, port calls, and HADR missions.
    *   It does NOT allow for permanent basing of troops but stipulates a maximum of 3,000 troops for mutually agreed engagements. A key aspect is access to Russian military facilities in the **Arctic**, expanding cooperation in new navigation routes due to global warming.

*   **Current Issue:** 📈 India-U.K. trade deal to take effect from July 15 (Page 3, 13)
*   **Main Points:**
    *   The long-awaited **India-U.K. Free Trade Agreement (FTA)** is set to take effect from July 15.
    *   The deal involves significant tariff reductions on various products and aims to boost **bilateral trade** and **economic cooperation**.
    *   The agreement includes provisions on goods, services, investment, intellectual property rights, and customs procedures, signaling strengthened **strategic partnership** between the two nations.
