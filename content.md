## 🌍 Geography & Environment

*   **Current Issue:** 🌊 **Padma Barrage & Transboundary Water Issues (India-Bangladesh)**
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Bangladesh is constructing a new **Padma barrage** 180 km downstream of India's Farakka barrage to control the Padma (Ganga) river for water security.
    *   **Farakka Barrage's Impact:** Criticized by Bangladesh for seasonal water scarcity, altering Ganga's character, reducing freshwater flow, impacting groundwater, increasing salinity in the Sundarbans, and affecting navigation.
    *   **Environmental Concerns:** Barrages lead to loss of sediment, increased flood risk, and adverse effects on the **Sundarbans mangrove ecosystem** (requiring brackish water) and local livelihoods.
    *   **Treaty Context:** The 1996 Ganges Water Treaty expires in December 2026, raising concerns about future water-sharing agreements and the unpredictability of river flows.
    *   **Expert View:** Experts suggest smaller, localized check-dams might be more environmentally sustainable than large barrages, but require complex cross-country planning.
    *   **UPSC Relevance:** Transboundary river disputes, India-Bangladesh relations, environmental impact assessment of large projects, climate change (sea-level rise in Bangladesh), and sustainable water resource management.

*   **Current Issue:** 🚗 **Sustainable Tourism & Urban Congestion in Shimla**
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Shimla is experiencing unprecedented tourist influx, leading to severe **traffic congestion and parking challenges**.
    *   Local police have implemented a new traffic management strategy, including "green channels" for tourists and route diversions.
    *   The situation highlights challenges in **urban planning, infrastructure development**, and managing the environmental and social impact of mass tourism.
    *   **UPSC Relevance:** Sustainable tourism, urban governance, traffic management, and the ecological footprint of human activities in hill stations.
