## 🌍 Geography & Environment
*   **Current Issue:** 🌊 Dhaka's New Padma Barrage and Transboundary Water Management
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Bangladesh is constructing a **new barrage on the Padma River** (Ganga), located 180 km downstream of India's Farakka Barrage.
    *   The project aims to address **water scarcity during the dry season**, control floods, improve navigation, and enhance fishing opportunities in Bangladesh.
    *   However, this development raises concerns in India regarding its potential impact on **downstream water flow**, environmental effects like salinity intrusion and groundwater levels, and the overall management of shared transboundary rivers.
    *   The situation underscores the critical need for **bilateral cooperation** and agreements between India and Bangladesh on water resource management.

*   **Current Issue:** 🇮🇷🇺🇸 Iran-U.S. Framework Deal and its Global Impact
*   **Main Points:**
    *   A 14-point framework agreement between Iran and the U.S. is expected to be signed soon in Geneva, outlining a **nuclear pledge and financial relief**.
    *   Iran has pledged **never to produce nuclear weapons** and to maintain a status quo on its nuclear program, including down-blending highly enriched uranium under supervision.
    *   The U.S. agreed to facilitate the release of frozen Iranian assets, **lift sanctions** on Iranian crude oil exports, banking, insurance, and transportation services, and plan for Iran's economic development ($300 billion).
    *   This deal is expected to remove restrictions on tankers and unlock banking/insurance access for Iranian oil trade, potentially influencing **global oil prices**; however, Indian shipowners remain cautious and are seeking government support.
*   **Current Issue:** 🇮🇳🇷🇺 India-Russia Logistics Support Agreement (RELOS) Clarification
*   **Main Points:**
    *   The **Reciprocal Exchange of Logistics Agreement (RELOS)** between India and Russia, operationalized in January, is a foundational military cooperation agreement.
    *   It clarifies procedures for the reciprocal use of each other's bases/ports for supplies, repairs, and fuel during joint exercises, training, humanitarian assistance, and port calls.
    *   Crucially, the agreement **does NOT permit permanent stationing of troops** (e.g., 3,000 Russian troops on Indian soil), contrary to some social media claims; it is similar to LSAs India holds with other countries.
    *   A key aspect is that RELOS grants India **access to Russian military facilities in the Arctic**, aligning with expanding cooperation in this strategically important region.
*   **Current Issue:** 🇮🇳🇬🇧 India-U.K. Trade Deal Implementation
*   **Main Points:**
    *   The India-U.K. bilateral trade deal is confirmed to take effect from **July 15, 2026**.
