## 🌍 Geography & Environment

*   **Current Issue:** 💧 Transboundary River Management: Bangladesh's Padma Barrage
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Bangladesh has initiated the construction of the new **Padma barrage**, located 180 km downstream of India's Farakka barrage, to control the Padma river (Ganga in Bangladesh) and address seasonal water scarcity.
    *   The project, estimated to cost ₹39,170 crore, aims to store 2,900 million cubic meters of water, serving 6.5 crore people in southwestern and northern Bangladesh.
    *   Experts highlight the **environmental costs** of such large barrages, including reduced groundwater recharge, increased salinity in critical ecosystems like the Sundarbans (affecting mangroves and livelihoods), altered silt flow, and heightened flood risks.
    *   The project also carries **geopolitical implications**, particularly concerning water-sharing treaties with India (like the 1996 Ganges Water Treaty and the pending Teesta River Treaty) and the increasing influence of China in regional infrastructure projects.
