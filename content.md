## 🏛️ Polity & Governance

*   **Current Issue:** ⚖️ **UCC Bill to be tabled in M.P. Assembly in July**
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister Mohan Yadav announced that a **Uniform Civil Code (UCC) Bill** would be introduced in the upcoming Monsoon Session of the State Legislative Assembly, slated for July 20.
    *   A six-member high-level committee, headed by retired Supreme Court judge Justice Ranjana Prasad Desai, has been drafting the Bill and submitting its report within 60 days.
    *   This move follows Uttarakhand and Gujarat, which have already implemented or are in the process of implementing UCCs.

*   **Current Issue:** 🏞️ **Nicobarese tribal councils oppose draft election rules**
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Tribal councils from three different island groups in Nicobar have written to the local administration, rejecting the idea of Election Commission-conducted polls for their self-governance system.
    *   They argue that introducing a new election system could lead to "election rivalry, division, and conflict," interfering with their "traditional, time-tested, and consensus-based" existing governance methods.
    *   The proposed draft rules aimed to reorganise villages into constituencies, involving delimitation, voter rolls, and reservation of leadership for women, which the councils oppose.

*   **Current Issue:** 👩‍⚖️ **SC Bench to look into allegation of Registry misplacing case records**
*   **Main Points:**
    *   The Supreme Court took strong exception to an allegation that its Registry had misplaced a case file, stating such "inefficiency" warranted an inquiry.
    *   The Chief Justice of India (CJI) expressed concern over the functioning of the administrative wing and emphasized the need for accountability in judicial administration.
    *   This incident highlights concerns regarding the **efficiency and transparency** of judicial record-keeping, which can impact access to justice.

*   **Current Issue:** 🧑‍🌾 **Rural workers, activists call for repeal of new jobs Act**
*   **Main Points:**
    *   NREGA Sangharsh Morcha and other unions announced indefinite protests from July 1, demanding the repeal of the **Viksit Bharat-Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) (VB-GRAM G) scheme**.
    *   The new scheme is set to replace MGNREGA and promises 125 guaranteed work days, but activists argue that budgetary allocations suggest only 42 days of work per year.
    *   Concerns also include the unprecedented burden on States, which are required to contribute 40% of the programme cost.

## 🌍 Geography & Environment

*   **Current Issue:** 🌊 **Dhaka's new Padma barrage to reshape water power in the region**
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Bangladesh has signed off on the new 2.1-km-long Padma barrage to control the Padma river (Ganga in Bangladesh) and address seasonal water scarcity.
    *   The barrage, located 180 km downstream of India's Farakka barrage, is intended to store 2,900 million cubic meters of water for 6.5 crore people.
    *   Experts warn of **significant environmental costs**, including altered river ecology, reduced groundwater recharge, increased salinity in Sundarbans, and impact on livelihoods, which could also strain **India-Bangladesh water diplomacy**.
