## 🏛️ Polity & Governance

*   **Current Issue:** ⚖️ Uniform Civil Code (UCC) Bill
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister announced that a **UCC Bill** will be tabled in the upcoming Monsoon Session of the State Assembly.
    *   This move follows Uttarakhand's lead, making Madhya Pradesh another BJP-ruled state to initiate legislation for a Uniform Civil Code.
    *   The implementation of UCC, as envisioned in **Article 44** of the Constitution, remains a significant national debate impacting personal laws.

*   **Current Issue:** 🏫 L-G Orders Audit of Schools for POCSO Compliance
*   **Main Points:**
    *   The Lieutenant Governor of Delhi has ordered an **audit of all schools** in the capital to ensure strict compliance with the **POCSO Act** (Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act).
    *   This includes verification of CCTV cameras, functional internal committees, and mandatory background checks for staff.
    *   Aims to enhance **child safety** and prevent abuses in educational institutions.

## 🌐 International Relations (IR)

*   **Current Issue:** 🤝 India-Russia Logistics Agreement
*   **Main Points:**
    *   India and Russia have signed an agreement for **reciprocal access to military facilities** for logistics and maintenance.
    *   This pact allows warships and aircraft of both nations to refuel, undergo repairs, and restock supplies at designated bases.
    *   Enhances **defense cooperation** and interoperability, similar to existing agreements India has with countries like the US, France, and Japan.

*   **Current Issue:** ⛽ US-Iran Deal: Potential Easing of Sanctions
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Discussions are underway between the US and Iran for a potential deal that could **lift restrictions on Iranian oil tankers, banking, and insurance access**.
    *   Aims to facilitate Iranian oil exports, potentially **increasing global oil supply** and impacting energy markets.
    *   This move is part of broader efforts to revive the **2015 Iran nuclear deal (JCPOA)** and de-escalate regional tensions.

*   **Current Issue:** 🌊 Dhaka's New Padma Barrage Project
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Bangladesh is planning a **6.4 km barrage on the Padma River** (Ganges) to manage water resources, generate hydropower, and improve navigation.
    *   Concerns exist regarding potential **environmental impacts** on groundwater levels, fish migration, and water availability for downstream areas in India.
    *   Highlights the critical need for **regional cooperation** and joint management of shared transboundary rivers.
