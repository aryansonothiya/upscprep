## 🏛️ Polity & Governance
*   **Current Issue:** **Uniform Civil Code (UCC) in Madhya Pradesh**
    *   **Main Points:** The Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister announced that a Bill on the UCC would be introduced in the upcoming Monsoon Session of the Legislative Assembly (July 20-24) and is expected to be passed in the same session. A six-member high-level committee formed in April is working on the draft Bill, having toured various parts of the State. The public was also invited to share suggestions on a government portal.
*   **Current Issue:** **School Audit for POCSO Compliance in Delhi**
    *   **Main Points:** Lieutenant-Governor (L-G) Taranjit Singh Sandhu has ordered a comprehensive citywide audit of schools in Delhi to assess compliance with the provisions of the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act. The audit will cover public and private schools and include aspects like safety infrastructure, child-friendly complaint mechanisms, awareness programs, and training for staff.
*   **Current Issue:** **Anti-Defection Law & Legislative Assembly Speaker's Role**
    *   **Main Points:** The Tamil Nadu Assembly Speaker confirmed that four former AIADMK MLAs, facing disqualification proceedings for defying the party whip during a trust vote and later resigning, have submitted their written responses. The Speaker stated he would review the replies and make a decision. This highlights the Speaker's adjudicatory role in disqualification cases under the Tenth Schedule.
*   **Current Issue:** **ED Probes & Judicial Scrutiny**
    *   **Main Points:**
        *   The Enforcement Directorate (ED) questioned T. Veena, daughter of former Kerala CM Pinarayi Vijayan, for around eight hours in connection with the Cochin Minerals and Rutile Ltd. (CMRL) pay-off case, probing payments made to her now-defunct IT company.

Ltd. by the ED as "contrary to law" and ordered their release, citing the absence of summons under Section 50 of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA). *   **Current Issue:** **Errors in School Textbooks (Odisha)**
    *   **Main Points:** Odisha Chief Minister Mohan Charan Majhi ordered immediate corrective measures and action against officials responsible for numerous errors (reportedly 1,678) in school textbooks for Classes 1 to 8. A three-member panel will be constituted to probe the matter. This raises concerns about quality control in education material.

## 🌍 Geography & Environment
*   **Current Issue:** **New Padma Barrage in Bangladesh & Transboundary Water Issues**
    *   **Main Points:** Bangladesh has approved a new Padma barrage to control the Padma river (Ganga in Bangladesh) and mitigate seasonal water scarcity. The 2.1-km-long structure, downstream of India's Farakka barrage, aims to store 2,900 million cu. m of water for 6.5 crore people. While addressing water scarcity, experts warn of significant environmental costs due to altered river ecology, reduced groundwater recharge, increased salinity, and impacts on ecosystems like the Sundarbans. This project also has implications for India-Bangladesh water diplomacy, especially given the upcoming expiry of the 1996 Ganges Water Treaty.
*   **Current Issue:** **Managing Summer Tourist Rush (Shimla)**
    *   **Main Points:** Shimla police adopted a five-point strategy to manage the influx of 15 lakh vehicles in 45 days during the summer tourist rush. Measures include deploying additional force, strict surveillance, mapping alternative routes, encouraging public transport, and using interceptor vehicles for traffic regulation. This highlights urban management challenges in popular tourist destinations.

## 🌐 International Relations (IR)
*   **Current Issue:** **Modi-Trump Bilateral Meeting (G7 Summit sidelines)**
    *   **Main Points:**
        *   PM Narendra Modi and U.S. President Trump met on the sidelines of the G7 Summit in France.
        *   **Indian Seafarers' Safety:** PM Modi raised the issue of the safety of Indian seafarers working on global maritime trade routes, including the Strait of Hormuz, following the killing of three Indian seafarers in a recent U.S. strike.
        *   **West Asia Peace & Strait of Hormuz:** Modi commended Trump's efforts for peace in West Asia and stressed the importance of keeping the Strait of Hormuz open for global economy.
        *   **Trade Deal:** Trump mentioned the U.S. and India were "very close" to finalising a trade deal, calling Modi a "very tough" negotiator.
*   **Current Issue:** **Iran-U.S. Framework Deal (Nuclear Pledge & Financial Relief)**
    *   **Main Points:** A 14-point framework agreement between Tehran and Washington is expected to be signed, outlining Iran's pledge never to produce nuclear weapons and maintain a status quo on its nuclear program. The U.S. has agreed to facilitate the release of frozen Iranian assets and create a plan for Iran's economic development ($300 billion), including lifting sanctions on products, services, crude, banking, and naval blockade. This signifies a potential de-escalation of tensions and economic normalisation.
*   **Current Issue:** **India-U.K. Trade Deal**
    *   **Main Points:** The India-U.K. trade deal is set to take effect from July 15, as differences have been resolved. It aims to reduce non-tariff barriers, simplify customs procedures, and eliminate tariffs on 90% of British exports to India and 98% of Indian exports to the U.K. This agreement seeks to boost bilateral trade and strengthen economic ties.
*   **Current Issue:** **India-Russia Logistics Agreement (RELOS)**
    *   **Main Points:** The Reciprocal Exchange of Logistics Agreement (RELOS) between India and Russia was operationalized in January. This foundational military cooperation agreement defines procedures for reciprocal use of bases/ports for supplies, repair, and fuel during joint exercises, training, port calls, and HADR missions. It *does not* allow permanent stationing of troops but facilitates logistical support for mutually agreed engagements. A key aspect is access to Russian military facilities in the Arctic, aligning with expanding cooperation in that region.
