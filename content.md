## 🏛️ Polity & Governance
*   **Current Issue:** ⚖️ Uniform Civil Code (UCC) Push & Central Bank's Fiscal Role
*   **Main Points:**
    *   **UCC in Madhya Pradesh:** Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister announced that a Bill on the Uniform Civil Code (UCC) will be introduced in the State Legislative Assembly's Monsoon Session in July. A six-member committee is currently drafting the Bill after studying models from other BJP-ruled states like Uttarakhand and Gujarat.
    *   **RBI's Growing Fiscal Role:** The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) approved a record surplus transfer of **₹2.87 lakh crore** to the Union government for FY26. This significant transfer, while consistent with the Economic Capital Framework, highlights the RBI's increasing fiscal importance, raising questions about central bank independence, fiscal centralization, and the lack of automatic state share in such non-tax revenue transfers.
    *   **POCSO Compliance Audit:** The Delhi Lieutenant-Governor (L-G) ordered a comprehensive, citywide audit of all schools to assess compliance with the provisions of the **Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act**, aiming to enhance child safety and institutional accountability.

## 🌍 Geography & Environment
*   **Current Issue:** 🏞️ Transboundary Water Management – Padma Barrage
*   **Main Points:**
    *   **Bangladesh's Padma Barrage:** Bangladesh has signed off on a new **2.1-km Padma barrage** (on the Ganga in Bangladesh) located 180 km downstream of India's Farakka Barrage. The project aims to control the Padma river and abate seasonal water scarcity, storing 2,900 million cubic meters of water.
    *   **Environmental & Bilateral Concerns:** Experts warn of significant environmental costs, including reduced groundwater recharge, increased salinity, riverbank erosion, and adverse impacts on the Sundarbans ecosystem due to altered sediment and freshwater flow. This project also holds implications for India-Bangladesh relations, especially concerning the 1996 Ganges Water Treaty and ongoing river-sharing disputes.

## 🌐 International Relations (IR)
*   **Current Issue:** 🤝 India's Strategic Defence & Economic Engagements
*   **Main Points:**
    *   **India-Russia RELOS Agreement:** The **Reciprocal Exchange of Logistics Agreement (RELOS)** between India and Russia has been operationalized. This foundational military cooperation agreement allows for the reciprocal use of each other's bases and ports for supplies, repair, and fuel during joint exercises, training, humanitarian assistance, and disaster relief (HADR) situations, including access to Russian facilities in the Arctic. It does *not* provide for permanent basing of troops.
    *   **Iran-U.S. Framework Deal:** A 14-point framework agreement between Iran and the U.S. was published, outlining Iran's pledge to never produce nuclear weapons and detailing potential financial relief from the U.S. (lifting sanctions, naval blockade withdrawal, and asset release). The deal, expected to be signed in Geneva, has significant implications for global geopolitics and energy security.
    *   **India-U.K. Trade Deal:** The bilateral trade deal between India and the United Kingdom is set to take effect from **July 15**. The agreement aims to significantly boost trade in goods and services, streamline customs procedures, and potentially remove tariffs on a large percentage of goods, strengthening economic ties between the two nations.
