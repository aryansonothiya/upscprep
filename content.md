## 🌍 Geography & Environment

*   **Current Issue:** 🌊 Transboundary River Management & Water Scarcity
*   **Main Points:**
    *   Bangladesh is constructing a new Padma barrage on the Ganga river, 180 km downstream of India's Farakka barrage, to control water flow and address seasonal scarcity.
    *   The project aims to provide water for 6.5 crore people but raises concerns about **significant environmental costs**, including increased salinity, riverbank erosion, and adverse impacts on the Sundarbans ecosystem.
    *   The initiative highlights the complexities of **transboundary water treaties** (like the expiring 1996 Ganges Water Treaty) and the broader challenges posed by large-scale infrastructure projects on shared river basins.
